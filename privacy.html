Who we are

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and name, and optionally your company and job title. Passwords are held by our sign-in provider in hashed form; we never see them.
  • What you follow: the neighbourhoods on your watchlist and any places you save (a label, postcode, its map location and the walking distance you chose), and whether you want the Monday Brief by email.
  • Your subscription: your plan, its status and renewal date, and a Stripe customer reference. Stripe collects your card, billing name, address and any tax ID; we never see your full card number.
  • Team plans: the email addresses of colleagues you invite, and, if you add them, your brand name, a link to your logo and a Slack webhook address for the Monday Brief.
  • One-off reports: the postcode and distance you bought a report for. Stripe collects the email and payment details at checkout.
  • Pilots and feedback: if your firm takes part in a pilot or founding partner programme, the details of the arrangement and our notes of the feedback you give us on calls or by email.
  • Free sample brief: if you ask for a sample brief without an account, your email address, the neighbourhood you chose, the page you asked from, and whether you also want occasional updates.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.

What we don’t keep

Postcodes and place names you type into the postcode check or the comparison tool are sent from your browser to postcodes.io to find the location. We don’t store them unless you save the place to your account; our anonymous counts (below) record only the postcode district, such as “SE1”. We don’t record which pages you visit against your account.

If we contact you about Terrayne

We may email or message people at property developers, planning consultancies and similar firms, using business contact details that they or their firm have published, such as on a company website or a professional profile, because we think Terrayne is relevant to their work (legitimate interests). We keep a simple record of who we contacted, when, and what was said. Every message tells you how to opt out: reply to say so, or email hello@terrayne.co, and we won’t contact you again. We keep only a note of your opt-out, so that we respect it.

Why we use it

To provide your account, the Monday Brief, alerts and reports (to perform our contract with you); to take payment and keep accounting records (contract and legal obligation); to send a sample brief you ask for, and occasional updates only if you tick the box (consent, which you can withdraw any time with the link in every email); to understand which parts of the site people use, from anonymous counts (legitimate interests); and to keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: account database and sign-in.
  • Stripe: payments and invoices.
  • Resend: sending the Monday Brief and account emails.
  • Cloudflare: privacy-friendly visit counts (Web Analytics) and bot protection on the sign-in form (Turnstile).
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the website. Like any web host, it handles your IP address when you load a page.
  • jsDelivr: delivering a software library used by the sign-in pages.
  • postcodes.io: turning postcodes and place names into map locations.
  • Slack: only if a Team owner connects a Slack channel for the Monday Brief.
  • Anthropic (Claude): the online workspace where we keep our business records, such as our list of customers, pilots and firms we have contacted.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK–US data bridge.

Storage in your browser

We use your browser’s storage only to keep you signed in, count your free postcode checks, remember which city you last viewed, and remember the plan you picked while you sign up. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and visits. It sets no cookies, does not track individual visitors and does not fingerprint your device.

Links to other websites

Briefs, reports and the planning feed link to council records through a web search (Google) and to official data sources. When you click one, you leave Terrayne and that website’s own privacy policy applies. We don’t pass your account details with the link. PDF downloads are made in your browser; the brief or report isn’t sent to anyone else to create them.

We also keep anonymous counts of a few actions, such as checking a postcode, clicking “Start free” or starting checkout. Each count records only the action, the page, a short detail (such as the plan or the postcode district) and the website you came from. It contains no name, email, IP address or account link, and uses no cookies or browser storage.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Deleting your account from your profile page cancels any subscription straight away and deletes your account, watchlist, saved places and team links. Sample brief sign-ups are kept until you ask us to stop, using the link in the email or by emailing us; we then keep only a note that the address asked not to be emailed. Anonymous counts are kept for up to two years. Records of firms we have contacted, and notes from pilots and feedback calls, are kept for up to two years after our last contact, unless you become a customer. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as tax law requires, and email providers keep delivery logs for a short period.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict how we use it. You can change most details and delete your account yourself; for anything else, email hello@terrayne.co. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Terrayne is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and, for significant changes, tell account holders by email.
