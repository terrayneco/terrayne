Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Draft: to be reviewed and completed with Terrayne’s legal entity details before paid plans launch.

What we collect

When you create an account: your name, email address, and optionally your company and job title; the neighbourhoods on your watchlist; and your email preferences. If you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment details. We receive your billing name, address and subscription status but never your full card number.

Why we use it

To provide your account and the Monday brief (performance of a contract), to bill you (contract and legal obligation), and to keep the service secure (legitimate interests). We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Supabase (account database and sign-in), Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery) and GitHub (website hosting). Some of these providers may process data outside the UK under appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Deleting your account from your profile page removes your profile and watchlist immediately. Billing records are kept by Stripe for as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to or restrict its use. Most of this you can do from your account; otherwise email hello@terrayne.co. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

We use only essential storage needed to keep you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
