3. Not advice

Terrayne is information for research. It is not investment, financial, legal, valuation or planning advice. Scores describe how much is changing in an area; they are not predictions of prices or returns. Check planning records with the relevant council, and take professional advice, before making decisions.

4. Accuracy

Our data comes from official sources, chiefly the GLA Planning London Datahub, TfL, the Office of Rail and Road, the ONS and HM Land Registry. These sources can be incomplete, delayed or later corrected, and statuses change. Decisions appear when the council records them, which can be some days after the decision itself. The “why it matters” notes are generated automatically from each application’s own figures and local data; they are context, not a professional assessment. We show a data confidence rating and the date of each update, but we do not guarantee that scores, statuses or figures are complete or current. How the scores work is explained on the methodology page.
