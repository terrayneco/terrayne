The Development Momentum Index

London first. More UK cities to follow.

One score for how fast a neighbourhood is changing.

A score from 0 to 100 for how fast a neighbourhood is changing, built from seven signals across five official sources: planning filings, station use, population and sale prices. A score of 90 means more momentum than about 90% of London. In a back-test, areas scoring 80+ grew their population +5.3% against +1.6% for areas under 40. How it works

Questions

Good to know.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Anyone can check 3 postcodes a month, see London’s top 10 neighbourhoods and compare them, and preview the planning feed and office-to-home (Class MA) tracker, with no card needed.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Pro and Team start with 14 days free, and you don’t need a card to start. If you haven’t added a payment method by the end of the trial, you simply move to the free plan.

Is this investment advice?

No. Terrayne shows what’s been filed, built and measured in an area, from official sources, so you can do your research faster. Scores describe activity, not future prices or returns. Always do your own checks before buying, building or investing.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, in a couple of clicks from your account. You keep access until the end of the month or year you’ve paid for, and you won’t be charged again.

Where does the data come from?

Only official open data: planning applications from the GLA’s Planning London Datahub, station use from TfL and the Office of Rail and Road, population from the ONS, and sale prices from HM Land Registry. See exactly how the score works.

How up to date is it?

Planning applications are refreshed every Monday. Station use, population and sale prices update when the government publishes them, which is monthly to yearly. Every page shows when its data was last updated.

Which areas are covered?

All of Greater London, which is where Terrayne is focused. Greater Manchester is available as an experimental beta with its own method, and more UK cities will follow once London is right.

What’s in the £12 Postcode Report?

Everything planned within a 5, 10 or 20-minute walk of the postcode, with council references; how many homes and new residents that means; the area’s Momentum score and price headroom; and a short summary of demand, risks and the local market, as a downloadable PDF. See a sample.

Can my team share an account?

Each person needs their own login. Team covers up to 5 people with a shared watchlist and team alerts. Talk to us for larger teams.

Is my payment secure?

Payments are handled by Stripe, and Terrayne never sees or stores your card details.
