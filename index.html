The problem

33 boroughs. 33 portals. Hours of reading.

London planning data is scattered across borough portals and buried in bureaucratic language like Class MA prior approvals and Section 106 agreements. Manual analysis is slow, and by the time it’s done the opportunity has moved.

Fragmented

Every authority publishes differently. No single, consistent view of a city that’s changing everywhere at once.

Opaque

Dense jargon hides what a filing actually means for a high street, a rent roll or a development appraisal.

Too slow

Analysts spend hours on triage instead of decisions, and the signal arrives after prices have already adjusted.

How it works

From filings to a decision.

The product isn’t the data, it’s the interpretation of the data. Terrayne connects planning, pipeline and footfall signals to explain where places are heading, built on public Greater London Authority and UK Government planning data.

01

Ingest

Planning applications from across London are pulled in automatically, filtered for major developments (10+ units) and Class MA conversions.

AI turns raw filings into plain-English commercial takeaways and scores every submarket on momentum.
